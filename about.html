Our Origin Story

Pioneering Career-First Beauty Training

Founded in 2012, BeautyPro began with a clear purpose: to bridge the gap between traditional trade school requirements and the high-energy reality of modern metropolitan salons and medical spas.

Our 15,000 sq. ft. flagship campus features Italian hydraulic styling stations, private clinical treatment suites, and a dedicated digital portfolio photography studio — ensuring you build clinical confidence before graduation.

The Journey

How BeautyPro Bloomed

A decade of educational innovation and graduate achievements in professional beauty.

2012

The First Styling Studio

BeautyPro launched with 10 styling stations and a passionate mission to elevate cosmetology training into a respected creative profession.

2016

State Board Accreditation & Expansion

Earned full California State Board licensing accreditation and achieved a 98% first-time examination pass rate across all graduating cohorts.

2021

Clinical Esthetics & MedSpa Suites

Added 6 private medical-grade skincare suites, high-frequency equipment, and European facial apparatus to serve expanding wellness industries.

2026

Digital Student Dashboard & 5,000 Alumni

Over 5,000 successful alumni now lead salons, spas, and bridal agencies globally, supported by our interactive hours and exam tracker portal.
